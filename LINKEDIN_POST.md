🎓 Excited to share my latest project: Student Performance Intelligence and Academic Analytics! 📊⚡

In many educational environments, student marks and attendance logs remain siloed in messy spreadsheets. This makes it slow to identify students needing intervention, prone to human error, and inconsistent when handling tie-breaks and grade cutoffs.

To solve this, I designed and built an end-to-end, automated Python academic analytics pipeline that ingests raw student records, validates data constraints, calculates statistical distributions, and delivers executive terminal intelligence.

Here is an architectural breakdown of what makes this project robust:

🔹 Strict Data Integrity & Validation: Implemented schema bounds checking, uniqueness validation for student IDs, and range enforcement for marks and attendance [0.0, 100.0].
🔹 High-Performance Vectorization: Leveraged Pandas and NumPy (mean, median, and ddof=0 population standard deviation) with zero in-place mutations—ensuring functional immutability.
🔹 7-Tier Academic Grading Engine: Automated subject mastery checks and letter grade assignment (A+ down to F) with multi-subject pass/fail requirements.
🔹 Fair, Tie-Aware Ranking: Built a multi-key sorting algorithm that preserves all students tied at the 5th-rank cutoff rather than arbitrarily dropping contenders.
🔹 Independent Attendance Risk Intelligence: Automatically flags students falling below the 75% attendance threshold without distorting their academic merit evaluation.
🔹 Automated Statistical Commentary: Generates data-driven findings directly from processed metrics for academic coordinators.
🔹 100% Automated Test Coverage: Built a 20-scenario unit test suite using Python's `unittest` framework to verify every boundary condition, tie scenario, and invalid input.

📊 Key Insights from the 30-Student Cohort:
• Class Pass Rate: 83.33% (25 of 30 students passed all subjects)
• Class Mean Score: 63.57 | Median: 63.17 | Std Dev: 18.30
• Top Performing Subject: Statistics (Mean: 64.97 marks)
• Top Performing Department: Computer Science (Mean: 66.46 marks)
• Attendance Action Flag: 8 students (26.7%) identified for attendance support (< 75%)

Python 3.11+ | Pandas 2.0+ | NumPy 1.24+ | unittest | CSV (RFC 4180) | Matplotlib
